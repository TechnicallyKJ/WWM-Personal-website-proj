The Legend Begins

A World Torn by Chaos. A Name Yet Unwritten.

Step into tenth-century China during the turbulent Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period — an era of political intrigue, shifting power, and legendary battles that shaped the course of history.

You are a wandering sword master of uncertain origin. From the bustling imperial capital to the forgotten wilderness, every path conceals secrets. Your every choice shapes your legend — and your fate.

What Awaits You

A Living World Forged in Ink & Iron

Fluid Martial Combat

Master over 40 unique Mystic Arts drawn from authentic martial traditions — from the flowing grace of Tai Chi to the thunderous force of Lion's Roar. Every weapon feels alive.

Boundless Open World

Scale rooftops with fluid parkour, soar across miles on windstride, or descend into layered underground catacombs. 20+ distinct regions stretch to every horizon.

Your choices echo through the Jianghu. Cause chaos and face bounties. Walk a noble path and earn allies. Every action carries weight. Every reputation is earned.
